## Where it is

The library is inside the red brick house in the middle of the village. The door is usually left ajar; push it open and you'll face two walls of shelves reaching to the ceiling.

> A simple static map or screenshot is worth adding for first-time visitors.

If you're travelling from far away, it's a good idea to say hello on the [guestbook](/guestbook) before you come — the owner may be able to point you a clearer way.

## Opening hours

**Daytime, whenever you like.** There are no business hours, no gate, and no librarian — it's simply an open room.

One small request: late at night, please be considerate of the household's rest. If you'd like to come in the evening, it's best to ask first on the guestbook.

## How to borrow, how to return

- **Read on the spot** — find a place to sit and read as long as you like. No sign-in needed.
- **Take it home** — carry off any book you love, and bring it back to the shelf when you're done.
- **No time limits, no fines** — there's no due date and no late fee. We trust just one thing: that you'll bring it back.
- **Logging is optional** — there's a notebook on the shelf; if you like, jot down which book you took and when, so others know where it went. No need if you'd rather not.

## A little etiquette

Whether this library stays open for years depends on everyone who comes. Please treat it as your own study:

- return finished books to where you found them, so the next reader can meet them too;
- be gentle with the pages — no dog-ears, no marks;
- keep it quiet and tidy, and take your litter with you;
- if you have books to share, you're warmly welcome to [donate](/donate).
